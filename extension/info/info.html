How SleepyTube works

Drift off gently. Wake up softly.

Fade to Sleep

Play a video on YouTube, open SleepyTube and switch on Fade to Sleep. Then:

  1. Countdown: nothing changes for the time you pick (10 to 90 minutes).
  2. Fade: the sound fades smoothly over 10 to 30 minutes.
  3. Whisper: it stays very quiet (4% of where it started) for 10 to 30 minutes.
  4. Silence: the video is muted. It keeps playing, and SleepyTube never pauses it.
+10 / +20
Keeps the current step going longer. During the fade, it carries on from the current level, so it never gets louder.
Restart
Brings the starting volume back and starts the countdown again from now.
Cancel, or switching it off
Stops the fade and brings the video's sound back.
Changing the chips
Re-plans tonight straight away. Steps that have already started stay as they are.
If you change the volume yourself
SleepyTube stops fading rather than fight you, and offers Resume fade. If you change it before the fade begins, that becomes the starting volume.

Only the YouTube tab you started it on is affected. Closing that tab stops the fade (you'll see a notice). Other tabs, YouTube's own settings and your computer's volume are never changed.

Alarm

Switch on Alarm to play SleepyTube's own gentle chime at your wakeup time. It doesn't need the video, the internet or the YouTube tab.

Choosing the time
Use the calendar button for any time in the next 24 hours, or +10 / −10 to nudge it. If Fade to Sleep is on, the alarm has to be after the video goes silent.
Test ▶
Plays the chime for a few seconds, so you can check the volume and where it plays (speakers or headphones).
When it rings
The chime starts very quietly and rises over about 90 seconds. A window opens with Snooze 9 min and I'm awake; a notification has the same buttons.
If nobody answers
It stops by itself after 15 minutes.
Waking up doesn't turn the video back up
Switch off Fade to Sleep, or press Restore video sound in the wake window, when you're ready.

Limitations

SleepyTube cannot wake your computer from sleep. Keep Chrome open and the computer awake, or use a separate alarm as backup.

  • The alarm only rings while Chrome is running and the computer is awake.
  • Keep computer awake (in limitations in the popup) stops sleep from inactivity until you dismiss the alarm. The screen can still turn off. It uses more battery and can't override closing a laptop's lid.
  • If the computer was asleep at alarm time and wakes 10 or more minutes late, the alarm is skipped and you're told, rather than it going off hours late.
  • The alarm can't be heard if the computer is muted or the sound goes to unplugged headphones.
  • YouTube may pause on its own (for example, "Video paused. Continue watching?"). SleepyTube can't prevent that.

Privacy

Everything stays on this computer. SleepyTube has no account, server, analytics, tracking or ads, and makes no network requests. It never reads or stores video titles, addresses, history or audio. It only works on www.youtube.com, and only on the tab you choose.

Keyboard

Tab moves between controls; Space or Enter flips a switch or presses a button. In a row of chips, the arrow keys choose the next or previous value. In the wake window, Snooze has focus first, so a sleepy key press snoozes rather than dismisses.
