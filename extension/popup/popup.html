    Before you rely on the alarm

    SleepyTube cannot wake your computer from sleep. Keep Chrome open and the computer awake, or use a separate alarm as backup.

    • Leave Chrome running. Closing it switches the alarm off.
    • Keep a laptop's lid open and plug it in.
    • Check the computer isn't muted, and press Test to hear where the sound plays (speakers or headphones).
    • If the computer was asleep at alarm time, the alarm is skipped rather than ringing late.
    • For Fade to Sleep, keep the YouTube tab open. Closing it stops the fade.

    Keep computer awake until the alarm

    Stops sleep from inactivity until you dismiss the alarm. The screen can still turn off. Uses more battery, and can't stop sleep when the lid closes.
